# Pricing

KillSwitch is designed as a sellable tool for professional traders, pods and family offices.  The following tiers are indicative and subject to change as the product matures.  All tiers include access to updates and email support.  Prices are quoted in USD per user per month.

| Tier | Target user | Included features | Price |
|-----|-------------|------------------|------|
| **Solo** | Individual active traders with a small number of positions. | Persistent thesis ledger, configurable kill conditions, workflow engine, local data storage and CLI. | **$29** |
| **Pod** | Small trading teams of up to 5 users. | All Solo features plus shared thesis repository, team board, worker daemon with fixture and live providers, and email digests. | **$99** |
| **Enterprise** | Family offices or institutional pods requiring audit trails and custom integrations. | All Pod features plus access control, API integrations for market feeds, compliance report generation and priority support.  Pricing bespoke. | **Custom** |

## Free trial

A 14‑day free trial is available for the Solo and Pod tiers.  During the trial all features are enabled except enterprise integrations.

## Not included

KillSwitch does not provide brokerage connectivity, order management, trade execution, live market data feeds or personalised investment advice.  Users must supply their own data providers or fixtures and should consult a licensed professional for investment decisions.
