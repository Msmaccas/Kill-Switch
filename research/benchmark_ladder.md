## Key takeaways

* **Memory matters.**  Trading and research frameworks increasingly persist reasoning steps and decisions to allow checkpoint resumption and to avoid duplicate work.  The persistent memory file or shared memory repository is now a standard feature.
* **Agents specialise.**  Successful systems divide labour into roles such as analyst, screener, trader, risk manager, contradiction detector and reporter.  This informs KillSwitch’s own agent decomposition.
* **Audit trails are valuable.**  Compliance‑oriented tools emphasise structured reports, explainable logs and audit readiness.  KillSwitch adopts a similar philosophy for thesis invalidation: every drift or break event must be traceable to specific evidence with timestamps and confidence states.
* **Gaps remain.**  None of the surveyed tools enforces user‑defined kill conditions or review cadences.  They either focus on idea generation or high‑level memory of research conversations.  KillSwitch fills this gap by enforcing honest review discipline when markets move.  Its contribution is complementary to existing agentic research and compliance tools.
